About Eason Logistics

Built on reliability, run with discipline

Eason Logistics is a professional logistics and transportation service provider focused on the reliable movement of goods. From our base in Kanchipuram District, Tamil Nadu, we plan, coordinate and execute freight, warehousing and delivery operations with an emphasis on safety, efficiency and timely outcomes.

Every shipment is treated as a commitment — to the schedule, to the handling standard, and to the relationship we're building with the business behind it. That approach is what keeps partners with us for the long term.

  • Reliable, planned delivery
  • Safety-first handling
  • Efficient route planning
  • Transparent communication
